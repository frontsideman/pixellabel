# Character Identity — Alex Sekunau

## 1. Physical appearance

A European man in his late 30s to early 40s, with a mature, masculine appearance, medium-to-solid build and natural body proportions.

**Head and face:**
- Completely shaved or naturally bald head, with no visible hairstyle.
- Rounded, slightly elongated head shape.
- Broad forehead with subtle horizontal expression lines.
- Light skin with a naturally warm, slightly pink complexion.
- Oval-to-rounded face with a strong lower facial structure.
- Natural facial asymmetry and realistic skin texture.
- Moderately prominent nose, relatively straight with a rounded tip.
- Light gray-green eyes, slightly deep-set, with a calm, attentive gaze.
- Medium-density eyebrows, naturally shaped and not overly dark.
- Relaxed, confident facial expression, often with a subtle, friendly half-smile.

**Beard:**
- Full, dense beard covering the chin, jawline and cheeks.
- Dark brown beard with clearly visible gray and silver strands, especially around the chin and lower sides.
- Natural, slightly textured beard rather than a perfectly sculpted barber-style shape.
- Connected mustache, medium density, blending naturally into the beard.
- Beard is a defining characteristic and must remain consistent across all illustrations.

**Body:**
- Adult masculine physique, medium-to-stocky build.
- Broad shoulders and moderately solid torso.
- Natural adult proportions, not exaggerated muscular or athletic proportions.
- Realistic arms and hands.
- Confident, relaxed posture.

## 2. Clothing and accessories

**Default outfit:**
- Black or charcoal zip-up hoodie.
- Plain black or dark charcoal T-shirt.
- Dark trousers or black jeans.
- Gray or neutral-colored casual sneakers.
- Minimal accessories.

**Optional variations:**
- Dark lightweight jacket.
- Olive-green outdoor jacket.
- Plain T-shirt with dark trousers.
- Casual backpack.
- Black rectangular sunglasses with slightly rounded edges.
- Laptop featuring small developer-related stickers.

Sunglasses are optional. The character must remain recognizable without them.

Avoid flashy jewelry, oversized chains, tattoos, exaggerated streetwear and stereotypical gangster clothing.

## 3. Art style

Original illustrated 1990s California open-world video game promotional aesthetic, inspired by GTA: San Andreas.

**Rendering characteristics:**
- Strong, clean black ink outlines.
- Stylized comic-book facial shading.
- Semi-realistic anatomy and facial structure.
- Hard-edged shadows combined with soft gradient lighting.
- Warm skin tones with coral and golden sunset highlights.
- Rich but controlled contrast.
- Detailed beard texture with distinct dark and gray strokes.
- Professional illustration with a polished, slightly cinematic appearance.

The character should look like an illustrated version of a real person, not a generic game protagonist.

## 4. Personality and visual impression

The character represents an experienced Senior Frontend Engineer.

He should appear:
- Confident but approachable.
- Experienced and technically competent.
- Calm, independent and thoughtful.
- Creative without being theatrical.
- Casual, practical and authentic.

He is a software engineer, not a gangster, action hero, bodybuilder or fashion model.

## 5. Identity consistency — critical requirements

Use the same recognizable facial identity across every website section and illustration.

**Never change:**
- Bald head.
- General head shape and forehead proportions.
- Gray-green eyes.
- Nose structure.
- Dense dark beard with gray strands.
- Mature facial proportions.
- Natural medium-to-solid body build.

**Allowed variations:**
- Facial expression.
- Body position.
- Camera angle.
- Clothing.
- Sunglasses on/off.
- Lighting.
- Environment.
- Activity.

Preserve the same identity in close-ups, half-body portraits, full-body illustrations, profile views and three-quarter views.

When reference photographs are available, facial likeness takes priority over artistic exaggeration.

## 6. Negative prompt

Avoid:
- Full head of hair, buzz cut or receding hairstyle.
- Young-looking face or teenager-like features.
- Clean-shaven appearance.
- Thin beard, goatee-only beard or unnaturally black beard.
- Excessively angular jaw.
- Oversized nose or exaggerated facial features.
- Unrealistically muscular body.
- Slim fashion-model proportions.
- Generic Hollywood actor appearance.
- Cartoonish oversized head or hands.
- Heavy tattoos or gold jewelry.
- Different facial identity between scenes.
- Photorealistic face pasted onto a cartoon body.
- Inconsistent beard shape or hair color.
