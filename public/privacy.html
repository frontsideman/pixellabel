Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This page explains what happens with information when you visit pixellabel.com. It is a static portfolio: there are no accounts, contact forms or payments.

Who is responsible

The site is maintained by Aliaksandr Sekunau. For privacy questions, contact me via Telegram or LinkedIn.

What is collected

  • Analytics. The site uses Yandex Metrica, a web analytics service provided by Yandex. It may set cookies and collect the page URL, referrer, browser and device details, approximate location derived from the IP address, and interaction events such as clicks and scrolling. I use this information only to understand how the portfolio is used and to improve it.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request data, including IP addresses, as part of providing and securing the hosting service.
  • Local preferences. The site stores two small preferences in your browser: portfolio-motion-v2 (local storage, remembers the reduced-motion choice) and portfolio-visited (session storage, prevents the intro from replaying in the same tab). They are not sent to me.
  • Messages. If you contact me through Telegram or LinkedIn, the conversation is handled by that platform under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and choices

Yandex Metrica may set analytics cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Yandex also provides an opt-out mechanism. If you are in a region where analytics requires prior consent, please use your browser controls or the Yandex opt-out before continuing to browse.

Retention and sharing

Analytics data is retained according to Yandex Metrica's settings and policies. I do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by Yandex and GitHub as service providers described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to its processing. Contact me through one of the channels above. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated date at the top of the page will change too.
